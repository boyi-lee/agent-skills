# Taiwan Traditional Chinese News Style

## Language

- Use Traditional Chinese suitable for Taiwan readers.
- Prefer Taiwan newsroom vocabulary over Mainland Chinese usage.
- Keep foreign brand/product names in their official form when provided.
- Avoid unnecessary English when a clear Chinese term exists.
- Avoid bureaucratic filler and AI-sounding abstractions.

## Punctuation

- Chinese prose uses full-width Chinese punctuation.
- Use 「」 for ordinary titles/quoted terms when newsroom convention permits.
- Keep product/model typography when it is part of the official name.
- Do not randomly alternate Chinese and Western punctuation.

## Readability

Prefer:
- short to medium sentences
- explicit subjects when pronouns could be ambiguous
- concrete transitions based on chronology, contrast, or category

Avoid:
- stacked adjectives
- vague transitions
- slogan-like conclusions
- generic filler such as「值得一提的是」「在此背景下」「綜上所述」

## PR cleanup

Flag or rewrite unsupported phrases such as:
- 頂級
- 殿堂級
- 重磅
- 震撼
- 必吃 / 必買 / 必收
- 完美
- 極致
- 革命性

They may remain only when clearly attributed, part of a proper name, or editorially justified by supplied evidence.
