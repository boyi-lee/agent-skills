# Fact Preservation / Fact Lock

## Non-negotiable rule

Editing may improve expression, structure, emphasis, and readability. It must not invent or strengthen facts.

## Locked items

Preserve exactly unless the source explicitly supports a correction:
- names and titles
- organizations and brands
- dates and times
- locations
- prices and quantities
- percentages and rankings
- awards and certifications
- quotes
- quote speakers
- causal claims
- comparison claims
- exclusivity claims such as 全台唯一、首家、最大、首次
- uncertainty markers such as 預計、規劃、可能、截至目前

## Forbidden transformations

- 「預計開幕」→「正式開幕」
- 「品牌表示」→「證實」
- 「約 3,000 元」→「3,000 元」
- 「其中之一」→「唯一」
- paraphrasing a quote into a stronger claim

## Fact Lock procedure

Before rewriting, record:
1. entities
2. dates/time
3. numbers/prices
4. quotes
5. attribution
6. qualifiers
7. source-dependent claims

After rewriting, diff against the list.

## Handling missing information

Do not fill gaps from general knowledge unless the user explicitly requests research and the claim can be sourced. Mark missing fields for human confirmation.
