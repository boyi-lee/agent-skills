# Attribution

## Attribution is required when

- the claim is supplied by a company, organizer, government body, expert, or interviewee
- the statement contains evaluation, intention, forecast, cause, ranking, or exclusivity
- a fact cannot be independently established from the supplied material

## Preferred forms

- 品牌表示
- 業者指出
- 官方資料顯示
- 主辦單位表示
- XXX 說明

Use the most specific source available.

## Quotes

- Keep speaker identity clear.
- Do not merge separate quotes.
- Do not convert paraphrase into quotation marks.
- Do not make a quote more definitive than the source.

## Promotional source material

When the only source is a press release, avoid presenting subjective brand claims as the reporter's own judgment.
