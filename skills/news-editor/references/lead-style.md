# Lead Style

## Goal

The first 1–2 paragraphs should answer the highest-value reader questions without forcing readers through company history first.

## Default lead order

1. what happened
2. who/brand
3. when/where
4. concrete reader-relevant detail
5. essential context

## Lifestyle lead

A useful lifestyle lead often combines:
- launch/opening/reopening
- location/date
- signature new item or change
- price/promotion/booking information when central

## Avoid

- opening with generic praise
- long corporate history before the new event
- repeating the headline nearly verbatim
- burying the actual date, price, or location
