# Author Style Module: 吳文元（上報生活）

Status: observational, evolving style profile  
Source corpus: public Up Media search/author pages and recent lifestyle articles reviewed on 2026-09-18.

## Scope

This module is suitable mainly for:
- 美食
- 餐飲
- 品酒
- 旅遊
- 消費型生活新聞

Do not automatically apply it to politics, social affairs, investigations, or hard-news desks.

## Observed structural tendencies

### 1. Headline
Common pattern:
- recognizable brand / venue / product first
- immediate event verb: 開幕、推出、登場、進駐、重新開幕
- one or more concrete hooks: price, date, number of dishes, branch, discount, booking window
- often optimized for practical search intent

The headline frequently answers both「發生什麼」and「讀者能得到什麼資訊」.

### 2. Lead
The opening tends to:
- identify the brand or venue
- establish brief historical/status context
- quickly state the new event
- surface opening date, new item, price, promotion, or location early

### 3. Body architecture
Articles commonly use descriptive subheads that behave like reader questions or search intents:
- 有哪些新菜？
- 何時開幕？
- 有什麼優惠？
- 空間有什麼變化？
- 價格／訂位資訊是什麼？

This creates modular scanning rather than a single long narrative.

### 4. Information hierarchy
Typical order:
1. newest event
2. brand/venue background
3. product/menu/detail expansion
4. space, chef, concept, collaboration, or feature
5. practical information
6. price / hours / address / booking / promotion

### 5. Density
- High proper-noun density
- High numeric density
- Frequent exact prices, dates, counts, branch/location details
- Reader utility is prioritized over literary transitions

### 6. Tone
- energetic lifestyle-news tone
- accepts some promotional vocabulary when traceable to brand positioning
- mixes straightforward reporting with consumer-oriented hooks
- often uses exclamation marks or strong headline punctuation more freely than hard-news desks

### 7. Search-oriented packaging
Repeated useful entities in subheads and paragraphs help search visibility:
- brand
- store/restaurant name
- product name
- location
- price
- opening date
- offer period

## Editing rules derived from the pattern

When asked to use this style:
- strengthen practical information hierarchy
- use descriptive subheads
- move date/price/location/booking details earlier when important
- retain brand and product names consistently
- make the article scannable
- prefer concrete specifics over abstract adjectives

## Guardrails

Do not copy distinctive phrases from existing articles.
Do not reproduce article passages.
Do not inherit promotional claims automatically.
Do not use this module as evidence that a claim is true.
Fact Lock and attribution rules always override stylistic similarity.
