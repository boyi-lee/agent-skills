# Headline Style

## Headline job

A headline should let the reader identify:
- the subject
- what changed
- why it matters now
- one or two concrete hooks

## Preferred building blocks

Subject / brand + action or event + concrete benefit or novelty + time/price/availability when useful.

## Rules

- Front-load the strongest verified news point.
- Favor specific nouns, numbers, dates, prices, locations, or availability.
- Avoid cramming every detail into one headline.
- Do not add superlatives that are absent from the source.
- If using a question-like promise such as「完整菜單」「預約時間一次看」the article must actually deliver it.
- Keep SEO-relevant proper nouns intact when they are central.

## Lifestyle-news variant

For consumer/lifestyle news, useful hooks include:
- opening date
- branch/location
- price
- limited period
- new menu/product
- booking method
- discount
- exclusivity, only when verified

Do not confuse clickability with sensationalism.
