# Numbers, Dates, Names

## Numbers

- Preserve supplied numeric values exactly.
- Keep units attached consistently.
- Do not silently normalize a price, percentage, serving count, capacity, or ranking if doing so could change meaning.
- For consumer news, surface price, date, store, booking, quantity, or discount when reader utility is high.

## Dates and times

- Prefer explicit dates when ambiguity is possible.
- Avoid relative terms such as 今天、明天、下週 when the article may be read later, unless publication context makes them intentional.
- Preserve timezone-sensitive schedules as supplied.

## Names

- Keep official brand, venue, person, product, and model names.
- On first mention, include full role/title when it materially helps identification.
- Do not invent Chinese translations for official foreign names.
