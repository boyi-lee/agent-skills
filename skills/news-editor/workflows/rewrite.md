# Workflow: Rewrite

1. Build Fact Lock.
2. Define the actual news point in one sentence.
3. Rebuild headline around subject + change + concrete reader value.
4. Rewrite lead to surface newest information first.
5. Group body into logical sections.
6. Add descriptive subheads only when they improve scanning.
7. Consolidate repeated background.
8. Preserve all sourced qualifiers and attribution.
9. Move practical details such as date, price, address, booking, and promotion to useful positions.
10. Final diff against Fact Lock.
